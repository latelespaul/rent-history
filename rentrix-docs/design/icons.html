Rentrix design · v1

Icon set

Original set drawn for Rentrix. Source: icons/icons.js · files: icons/<name>.svg · sprite: icons/sprite.svg.

Rules

  • 24 × 24 grid, 2 px safe padding, artwork inside 20 × 20.
  • One stroke: 1.75 px, round caps, round joins. No fills (the star is filled by CSS only for its "on" state).
  • Colour is always currentColor; the icon takes the text colour of its parent.
  • Sizes: 16 (inline with small text), 20 (default, buttons and lists), 24 (headers, empty states). Never scale strokes.
  • An icon next to a text label is decorative (aria-hidden). An icon-only button needs an aria-label.
  • Role icons: landlord = person under a roof, tenant = person in a doorway, tenancy = house with a check (a confirmed tenancy).
