The problem

Renting runs on trust nobody can check.

You hand over a deposit or a set of keys to a stranger, based on a broker's word and a 15-minute visit.

Tenants get burned

Deposits kept without a reason, repairs ignored for weeks, surprise rent rises at renewal.

Landlords take a gamble

Rent paid late, homes handed back damaged, and no way to check a tenant before move-in.

No record anyone trusts

Group-chat hearsay and listing-site stars vanish. Nothing follows a landlord or a tenant to the next rental.

Doorway Living keeps that record. Each side rates the other after a real tenancy, and the rent that was paid stays on the home's page.

How it works

Three steps, a few minutes.

  1. Step 1

    Sign in with Google

    Pick your side: tenant, landlord or both. Browsing homes, rents and reviews needs nothing more.

  2. Step 2

    Verify your phone, add your rental

    A one-time code to your phone lets you post. Add the home you rent or rent out, and the rent paid.

  3. Step 3

    Rate each other

    Tenants rate the home and the landlord; landlords rate the tenant. The next person reads it before they sign.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Write to us from the footer.

Do I need an account to look around?

No. Anyone can browse homes, rents and reviews. Sign in with Google when you want to add a home, a tenancy or a review.

Who can post a review?

A tenant or landlord with a verified phone number, about a tenancy they add. Tenants rate the home and the landlord; landlords rate the tenant.

What does the green tick mean?

Our team has checked that user, home, tenancy or review. Posts show at once as "Not verified yet" until then.

Will others see my Aadhaar, PAN or phone?

No. Others see only a badge such as "ID verified". Aadhaar and PAN are optional.

Can a landlord reply to a review?

Yes. Landlords can answer any review of them or their home, in public, under the review.
