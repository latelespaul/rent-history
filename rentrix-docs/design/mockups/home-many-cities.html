How trust works

1. Both sides confirm the tenancy

Landlord or tenant records the tenancy: property, dates, rent. The other side confirms. Strangers cannot rate anyone.

2. Reviews unlock together

Landlord and tenant reviews stay hidden until both are in, or 14 days pass. No revenge reviews.

3. Real rent history

Rent that both sides confirmed builds the property's history. No tenant names on it, ever.
