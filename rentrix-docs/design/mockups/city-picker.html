Component · CityPicker

City picker

One component for Home, Search, onboarding (home city), the property form and admin filters. It lists the active cities from the API, filters as you type on name, alias and state, and works the same for 2 or 200 cities. No city is typed into the web code. D-20 · D-85

1 · Closed, a city chosen

Shows name + state. Selecting writes ?city=BLR; search only ever sends a code.

2 · Open, launch list (2 active)

3 · Typing an alias

"beng" finds Bangalore through its alias Bengaluru, and West Bengal's Kolkata through its state: prefix of any word, case-insensitive. D-82

4 · No match

5 · Open, 9 active cities

7 of these rows are illustrative; activating a city is one admin action on a DB row (Admin · Cities). D-83

Behaviour

  • Keyboard: type to filter, ↑/↓ to move, Enter to choose, Esc to close. ARIA combobox pattern with a listbox; the active option is announced.
  • Data: useCities() loads GET /cities once (cached); filtering is client-side over code, name, aliases and state. ?q= is used only if the list is ever paged.
  • Copy under the picker and in the footer is built from the same list: "" with 2–3 cities, "" with more. The product never says "only".
  • Mobile: the popover takes the full width under the field; options stay 44 px tall.
