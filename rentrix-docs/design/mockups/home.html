How a review becomes trusted

1

Add the tenancy

Tenant or landlord enters property, dates and monthly rent.

2

The other side confirms

They accept the same details, or correct them. No confirmation, no review.

3

Both sides review

One review per direction per tenancy. Both are revealed together, so nobody retaliates.

4

Ratings add up

Scores roll up per landlord, tenant and property. Abuse can be reported.
